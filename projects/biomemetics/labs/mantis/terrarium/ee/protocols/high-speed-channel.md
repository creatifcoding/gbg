# Complete high-speed channel protocol

Status: `UNVERIFIED`; procedure draft only.

The fixture must include source launch, B27 rail pogo interface, carriage
routing, B50 binder handoff, binder launch, selected serializer/deserializer,
and representative power/ground return. Characterize both new and worn parts
under clean, humid/contaminated, skewed, and bounce conditions.

Select ADI-supported 3/6 Gbps limits before testing. Capture TDR, S-parameters,
return/insertion loss, eye, BER, training/recovery, ESD behavior, temperature,
contact resistance, contamination, and cycle count. Simulation can reject a
geometry but cannot pass this physical gate.
