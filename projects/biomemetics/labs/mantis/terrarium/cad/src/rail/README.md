# Rail OCCT source

Working draft B. Maturity DRAFT.

The rail sits on the outside of the perimeter. Top-front (250 mm) and front-left vertical (500 mm) are independent routes. A carriage does not turn the corner. B21 is that declaration, not a corner electrical solid.

B51 is a captive end stop on both ends of each route. Service removal uses a 3.4 mm bore marked UNVERIFIED; no fastener PN is selected. B52 is the external access-slot guard. B19 lands are a conductor proxy inside the rail cavity. They must not enter animal or wet keep-outs.

Pinch-carriage, B27 motion, and B50 binder solids belong to #29. This leaf only keeps an untethered carriage motion envelope.
