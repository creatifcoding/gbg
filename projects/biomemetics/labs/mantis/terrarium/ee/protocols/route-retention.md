# Rail route retention protocol

Status: `UNVERIFIED`; cross-domain procedure draft only.

Fit B51 end stops to every independent route with the worst-case carriage and
binder mass/moment. Select proof-load, vibration, handling/drop, fast-roll
impact, fastener torque, thread-retention, wear-cycle, and damaged-fastener
limits before testing.

Acceptance: the carriage never leaves the route or exposes the animal-side
boundary; normal tool-free operation cannot release B51; deliberate service
requires the specified M3 tool action; and the stop remains inspectable and
replaceable. Record pre/post dimensions, fastener condition, video, force data,
and independent review.
