## Notes on role assignment

- **trigger**: suitable for low-latency detection/alerting loops.
- **context**: suitable for enrichment, corroboration, and operator decision support.
- **archive**: suitable for backfill, historical analytics, model training, and post-incident review.

## Practical usage guidance

1. Do not use a single source as ground truth for high-consequence automation.
2. Pair at least one **trigger** source with one **context/archive** source in every pipeline.
3. Preserve source metadata (`source`, `endpoint`, `retrieved_at`, `license`, `version`) with every record for auditability.
